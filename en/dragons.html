Dancing Dragons

Wisdom in motion.

The Name

The name Dancing Dragons traces its origins back to a simple drawing made years before the club was founded. Inspired by the dragon featured on the former coat of arms of Hong Kong, a few subtle alterations transformed the traditional heraldic creature into something that appeared almost alive, as if it were dancing.

That image would eventually inspire the creation of the club itself. Rather than representing brute force or destruction, the dragon came to symbolise elegance, movement and mastery. The name reflects a philosophy where success is achieved through intelligence, precision and adaptability.

Founded on 16 August 2011, Dancing Dragons would grow into one of the most successful clubs in Belgian PManager history, combining sustained excellence with a distinctive identity of its own.

Philosophy

While many successful clubs build their reputation on power or intimidation, Dancing Dragons follows a different path. The club values wisdom, creativity and efficiency. Victories are earned not through chaos, but through control.

The dragon serves as the perfect symbol of this philosophy. Strong when necessary, but always composed, graceful and intelligent. Much like the mythical creature that represents the club, Dancing Dragons aims to overcome challenges through superiority of thought as much as superiority of talent.

The philosophy of the club can be summarised in three principles:

  • Move with purpose.
  • Win with intelligence.
  • Pursue excellence with elegance.

Success is important, but how it is achieved matters just as much. Dancing Dragons strives to combine competitive excellence with a style worthy of its name.

The Crest

The crest of Dancing Dragons reflects the character of the club.

At its centre stands a majestic Chinese dragon in an elegant, flowing pose. Unlike the aggressive dragons often found in mythology, this dragon embodies wisdom, refinement and controlled power. It appears to move effortlessly, symbolising both grace and confidence.

The dominant red background reflects passion, energy and ambition. Silver and white accents provide balance and sophistication, while the golden details celebrate the success achieved throughout the club’s history.

The skyline in the background serves as a subtle tribute to the original artistic inspiration that eventually led to the creation of the club. The Chinese characters represent wisdom and elegance, two qualities that perfectly capture the spirit of Dancing Dragons.

The crest communicates the same message as the club itself: strength expressed through grace rather than force.

History

Dancing Dragons was founded on 16 August 2011.

The club quickly developed into a competitive force and secured its first major trophy in Season 37, winning the Belgian Cup.

League success took longer to arrive. The club claimed its first Belgian championship in Season 56, establishing itself among the elite of Belgian PManager football.

Over time, Dancing Dragons evolved from a promising challenger into one of the dominant clubs of its era.

The first great period of glory came between Seasons 74 and 79. During those six seasons the club captured an extraordinary five league championships and five Belgian Cups, combining consistency with silverware on an almost unprecedented scale.

A second golden era followed from Season 91 to Season 95, when Dancing Dragons secured five consecutive league titles, confirming its place among the greatest clubs in the history of Belgian PManager.

Throughout its existence, the club has consistently combined success with a distinctive identity, remaining true to the principles that inspired its creation.

Honours

  • Belgian League Championships: 14
  • Belgian Cups: 10
  • League and Cup Doubles: 6

These achievements place Dancing Dragons among the most successful clubs Belgium has ever produced within PManager.

Legacy

Some clubs dominate through power. Others through tradition.

Dancing Dragons chose a different path, proving that elegance and ambition are not opposites.

The club's legacy is built upon the belief that greatness can be achieved without sacrificing style, and that wisdom remains one of the most powerful competitive advantages of all.
