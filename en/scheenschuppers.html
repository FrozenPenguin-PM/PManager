# Scheenschuppers **Quiet confidence. Lasting excellence.** The name *Scheenschuppers* originates from a Flemish dialect expression meaning "to kick someone's shins". Literally, it refers to kicking an opponent's lower legs. Figuratively, it describes someone who persistently disrupts, frustrates or outperforms others. When the club was founded in 2005, the name was little more than a playful linguistic joke. It was never intended to become the name of the most successful club in Belgian PManager history. Over the years, however, Scheenschuppers grew into an institution. What once started as a humorous choice became a name associated with success, consistency and longevity. A different name would no longer feel right. The club's identity has become inseparable from the name under which all those victories were achieved. Founded on **30 October 2005**, Scheenschuppers has grown into the most successful club in Belgian PManager history. Throughout generations of players, managers and rivals, one principle has remained unchanged: success is never the objective, it is the standard. ## Philosophy Scheenschuppers believes that true excellence requires more than talent alone. Success is built on discipline, long\-term planning and the ability to evolve. Every season presents new challenges, new opponents and new generations of players. Remaining at the top demands constant adaptation. The club's philosophy can be summarised in three principles: - Pursue excellence relentlessly. - Respect opponents without fearing them. - Remain humble regardless of success. The club does not define itself through rivalry or spectacle. Confidence comes from preparation, consistency and results. Scheenschuppers does not need to proclaim its status. Success accumulated over many seasons speaks for itself. ## The Crest The club crest reflects the identity of Scheenschuppers. At its centre stands an elegant Asian\-inspired figure. The figure does not represent aggression or dominance, but confidence, self\-control and natural authority. The gaze is calm and composed, reflecting a club that knows its value without the need to advertise it. The black and blue striped background represents the club colours and its footballing heritage. The silver details symbolise class and refinement, while the two gold stars recognise the exceptional accomplishments achieved throughout the club's history. The crest embodies the idea that greatness does not need to be loud. Confidence, when genuine, speaks for itself. ## History Scheenschuppers was founded on **30 October 2005**. The club's rise to prominence was anything but immediate. Although the foundations for future success were already present, the first major breakthrough did not arrive until **Season 21**, when Scheenschuppers lifted its first domestic cup. The first league championship followed in **Season 28** and was immediately backed up by a second title in **Season 29**, confirming that the club had established itself among the Belgian elite. However, sustained dominance remained elusive. After the second championship, supporters had to wait until **Season 47** before another league title arrived. In retrospect, that triumph would prove to be one of the most important moments in club history. Season 47 marked the beginning of the most dominant era Belgian PManager has ever witnessed. What followed was an unprecedented run of **eight consecutive league championships**, firmly establishing Scheenschuppers as the benchmark against which every other club would be measured. Over the years the club continued to add silverware and strengthen its legacy, combining consistency with an ability to renew itself across multiple generations of players and competitors. ## Honours **Belgian League Championships:** 22 **Belgian Cups:** 21 **League and Cup Doubles:** 11 More than the numbers alone, these achievements reflect over two decades of sustained excellence at the highest level of Belgian PManager football. ## Legacy More than trophies, Scheenschuppers is defined by continuity. Seasons come and go, rivals rise and fall, yet the club's identity remains unchanged. The objective has never been to become the biggest club. The objective has always been to deserve that status.
