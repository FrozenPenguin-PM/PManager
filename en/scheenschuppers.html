Scheenschuppers

Quiet confidence. Lasting excellence.

Introduction

The name Scheenschuppers originates from a Flemish dialect expression meaning "to kick someone's shins". Literally, it refers to kicking an opponent's lower legs. Figuratively, it describes someone who persistently disrupts, frustrates or outperforms others.

When the club was founded in 2005, the name was little more than a playful linguistic joke. It was never intended to become the name of the most successful club in Belgian PManager history.

Over the years, however, Scheenschuppers grew into an institution. What once started as a humorous choice became a name associated with success, consistency and longevity.

Philosophy

Scheenschuppers believes that true excellence requires more than talent alone. Success is built on discipline, long-term planning and the ability to evolve.

  • Pursue excellence relentlessly.
  • Respect opponents without fearing them.
  • Remain humble regardless of success.

The Crest

At its centre stands an elegant woman. The woman does not represent aggression or dominance, but confidence, self-control and natural authority.

The black and blue striped background represents the club colours and its footballing heritage.

History

Scheenschuppers was founded on 30 October 2005.

The first major breakthrough arrived in Season 21, when the club lifted its first domestic cup.

The first league championship followed in Season 28 and was immediately backed up by a second title in Season 29.

Season 47 marked the beginning of the most dominant era Belgian PManager has ever witnessed, including an unprecedented run of eight consecutive league championships.

Honours

  • Belgian League Championships: 22
  • Belgian Cups: 21
  • League and Cup Doubles: 11

Legacy

More than trophies, Scheenschuppers is defined by continuity.

The objective has never been to become the biggest club.

The objective has always been to deserve that status.
