The Beginning

Football management simulations have been a part of my life for more than two decades.

In the early 2000s, I came across a newspaper article about the online football management game Hattrick. Curious about the concept, I decided to give it a try and quickly became fascinated by the challenge of building a club over the long term.

My first club was called Saigon Snakes. The name reflected two personal interests that were already present at the time. The first was a growing fascination with Asia, and particularly Vietnam. The second was my Chinese zodiac sign, which inspired the animal part of the club name. Even then, my fondness for alliteration was already clearly visible.

While Hattrick was an enjoyable experience, the pace of the game was relatively slow. Over the years I explored several other football management simulations in search of something that better matched my preferred style of play. Most failed to hold my attention for long.

That changed when I discovered PManager.

I first learned about the game through discussions on an online forum. The description of a more tactical and strategic football manager immediately caught my attention. Still, my expectations were modest. After several disappointing alternatives, I viewed PManager as one final experiment.

On 30 October 2005, exactly one year after the launch of PManager itself, I started my journey in the game.

With a healthy dose of cynicism, I created a club named Scheenschuppers.

Discovering PManager

What followed would eventually become a journey spanning decades.

PManager proved to be exactly the type of game I had been searching for. Success was not simply about collecting the best individual players. Building the best team required planning, balance, tactical understanding and a long-term vision.

Season after season, Scheenschuppers improved. Over time, the club evolved from an unremarkable newcomer into the most successful club in Belgian PManager history.

Expanding the Story

As the years went by, new projects followed.

Dancing Dragons introduced a philosophy built on elegance, wisdom and sustained excellence.

Teethless Tigers was created as a development-focused project, prioritising player growth and long-term potential above immediate success.

Two additional experimental clubs would briefly appear along the way, but only Dragons and Tigers became lasting parts of the story.

A Tribute to the Beginning

The original Saigon Snakes have never been forgotten.

They remain an important part of the journey that ultimately led to PManager. Without the Snakes, Scheenschuppers, Dancing Dragons and Teethless Tigers would never have existed.

Although the club is no longer active and no longer forms part of the current project, it remains the starting point of everything that followed.

Three Clubs, Three Philosophies

Together, Scheenschuppers, Dancing Dragons and Teethless Tigers represent three different football philosophies, three distinct club identities and more than twenty years of football management experience.
